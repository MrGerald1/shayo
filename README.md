# Shayo

**Where Nigerians go to get something to say about what somebody is drinking.**

Shayo is a social drink-discovery app built around Nigerian drinking culture. The core unit is the **roast card** — a shareable 1080×1920 image with a witty take on a drink, designed to travel on WhatsApp.

## What it does

- **The Feed** — randomized drink roasts on the home screen
- **The Naming Loop** — "Who you wan roast?" — pick a friend's drink and get a card to send
- **Search** — filter by strength, venue, taste; aliases so "Henny" finds Hennessy
- **Share Cards** — 1080×1920 images, designed to be forwarded
- **The Shayo Index** — a reputation layer over Nigerian drink culture
- **Event Log** — writes from the first interaction, feeds act two (tab, ledger, radar)

## The thesis

Shayo is where Nigerians go to get something to say about what somebody is drinking. The roasts are the product and the card you send is the unit. Nigerian brewers, shut out of daytime broadcast by a watershed, need digital audiences of drinkers — that's the structural monetisation opportunity.
